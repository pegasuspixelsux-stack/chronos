# Design Reference: soldbydustin225.com

Extracted visual-design notes from a competitor real-estate site, for reference only.
Structural/stylistic observations — no copy, headlines, or body text reproduced.

## Color Palette

- Background: white / off-white primary surfaces
- Text: charcoal/dark gray for body copy, black for headings
- Accent: teal/turquoise for CTAs and interactive elements
- Cards: white background, subtle drop shadow for depth
- Price display: dark text on white, high contrast

## Typography

- Headings: sans-serif, medium-to-bold weight, title case or all-caps
- Body: sans-serif, lighter weight, standard reading size
- Property detail rows: smaller sans-serif, condensed for quick scanning
- Overall tone: modern/corporate, readability-first, minimal decoration

## Layout Structure

- Header: logo (left) · nav (center) · phone CTA (right)
- Hero: full-width image, text overlay, search bar embedded
- Body: featured-listings grid (3–4 columns) → secondary "just listed" grid
- Listing cards: vertical — image on top, details below

## Spacing & Density

- Generous whitespace between major sections
- Moderate internal card padding
- Compact info density inside each card despite generous outer spacing

## Card & Button Styling

- Cards: bordered or soft-shadow treatment
- Buttons: solid teal fill, rounded corners, minimal/text-based labels
- Photo-count badge: small circular indicator overlaid on the card image
- Price: bold and prominent

## Imagery

- Aspect ratio: square or near-square for listing photos
- Treatment: natural color, well-lit, professional real-estate photography
- Hero image: atmospheric/lifestyle or architectural, landscape orientation

## Distinctive UI Patterns

- Photo-count badge on listing thumbnails
- "Price reduced" indicator on adjusted listings
- "New" badge on recent listings
- Condensed spec line per card (beds · baths · sqft · acreage)
- Phone number repeated in header and nav (high-intent contact CTA)
- Search bar placed directly beneath/within the hero

---

**Note on relevance to Chronos:** this is a denser, teal-accented, corporate-brokerage aesthetic — the opposite pole from the warm-monochrome minimalist/editorial direction already chosen for Chronos (see the implementation plan). Treat this as a competitor reference, not a style to adopt wholesale, unless you want to reconsider the design direction.
